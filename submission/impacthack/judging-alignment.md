# Judging alignment

Prepared for **ImpactHack 2026** from the official pages reviewed October 1, 2026. These are tailored draft answers, not an already submitted entry.

- **Impact — 20%:** Identify students and electronics clubs as intended beneficiaries, without measured benefit claims.
- **Technical Execution — 20%:** Show strict schema validation and reviewed observations feeding the preserved graph engine.
- **Innovation & Creativity — 15%:** Explain why uncertain perception and deterministic reasoning have separate responsibilities.
- **Functionality — 15%:** Show graph correction and robust manual fallback; disclose failed live attempts.
- **Use of AI & Technology — 15%:** Describe the actual image-to-structured-observation API implementation and the current credit blocker.
- **Presentation & Demo — 15%:** Use a 2–3 minute product walkthrough with provenance and the pre/post-October-1 disclosure.

Source: [official judging criteria](https://impacthack26.devpost.com/).

Published rules require current high-school students; eligibility is unverified. Educational and technical categories are plausible framing, not an award claim. This is not hardware validation or an independently measured impact study.
