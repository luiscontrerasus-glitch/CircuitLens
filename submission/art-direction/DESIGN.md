# CircuitLens art direction

## Investigation

Visually inspected Squarespace homepage, template gallery, Wesley desktop and mobile, Novo, Reseda and Oranssi previews. Wesley uses one tightly cropped photograph, a compact header, and a large but plain typographic statement; its lower section switches to an asymmetric text/image composition. Novo lets a disciplined image grid carry the identity with very little introductory copy. Reseda uses a heavy grotesk masthead followed by asymmetric photography and supporting text. Oranssi varies image scale and aligns the headline to one side. The homepage establishes one dominant photographic moment before changing composition. These are principles, not layouts or assets to copy.

The current workbench gives a marketing heading and instructions more space than the model. Horizontal example controls compete with view controls, three equal panels compress the circuit, and diagnosis is displaced below the workspace. Source imagery should be an optional representation; findings should follow selection.

Functional guardrails: seven fixtures; unchanged engine and reference comparison; physical, source and schematic selection; terminal/value/type editing; polarity correction; confirmation; upload/camera input; manually reviewed perception; import/export/reset; actual consent/provenance; keyboard operation and reduced motion.

## Three directions reviewed

- **A — Component Index:** bold Archivo grotesk, ivory paper, asymmetrical specimen photography, vermilion action. Concrete and approachable. Selected, with more intentional headline line breaks than the initial prototype.
- **B — Inside Matters:** quiet serif, charcoal field, cross-section macro photograph. Strong material quality but too abstract and contemplative for the opening product explanation.
- **C — Working View:** tight utility grotesk and a dominant genuine diagram. Clear, but retains too much of the earlier programmatic visual identity.

Opening-screen prototypes and a complete workbench prototype were rendered and inspected before application edits. Desktop prototype establishes library/canvas/inspector. Mobile prototype revealed overly small SVG labels; implementation must use an adapted schematic coordinate layout, not shrink the desktop diagram.

## System

Marketing: Archivo variable grotesk, expressive 650–750 display weight, restrained 400 supporting text. Ivory #f3f0e9, charcoal #242323, gray #6a6966, vermilion #bd4326. Optical negative tracking only in display text. Asymmetric grid, actual component photograph, then one authentic software view and concise example index. No scroll narrative or diagram hero.

Application: same type family at practical 12–14 px sizes; 56 px navigation; collapsible 208 px charcoal library; flexible central canvas; 300 px contextual inspector. Source image, physical model and schematic occupy the same central workspace. Full circuit editing, report, perception review and help are progressively disclosed. Mobile starts with a readable circuit and moves the inspector below it. No introductory heading or instructional steps.

Motion: brief state/hover transitions, no scroll hijacking, no continuous marketing animation. Reduced motion removes transforms/transitions and defaults to schematic.
