# 30-second pitch

CircuitLens helps beginners understand why a breadboard circuit does not work. Review a photo, confirm its terminals, and let a deterministic graph engine check the build against an intended design.

It flags supported wiring faults and explains the evidence, electrical consequence, and correction. Seven generated experiments run through the live engine without cloud credentials.

The MVP uses manual photo interpretation, not automatic recognition. It is a working foundation for AI-assisted debugging, built around evidence and learning.
