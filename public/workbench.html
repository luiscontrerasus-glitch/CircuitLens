How breadboard terminals work

A1–E1 share a net; F1–J1 share a separate net. Rows 1–30 are isolated. VCC and GND are logical continuous rails. Verify split rails on your real board. LED terminal A means anode; B means cathode. Resistor A/B labels are matched to the reference.
