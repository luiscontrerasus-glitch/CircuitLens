Know the instrument

Clear about what it can see.

CircuitLens checks a confirmed model. It cannot measure physical continuity, inspect hidden contacts, or replace a circuit simulator.

Supported components and analysis

Wires, resistors, two-lead LEDs, and effective two-terminal switches. Series LED estimates assume a 2 V drop; voltage-divider estimates assume no load. Split rails and unsupported components need human interpretation.

Photo review and recognition limits

Manual photo review is available. Public AI recognition is disabled. In the documented evaluation, none of six real breadboard photos produced a complete verified Gemini reconstruction. Example circuits are supplied models, not AI-recognition results.

Keep your circuit and report

Export the netlist or analysis report as JSON. Work stays in browser memory until you export it; refreshing clears the session. Exports do not include uploaded images.
